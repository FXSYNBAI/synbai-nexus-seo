# SYNBAI X content calendar

The goal is consistent technical education and build-in-public evidence, not sensational performance marketing.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday — Research question:** one clear question about validation, costs or robustness.
- **Wednesday — Evidence post:** one dated result with dataset, assumptions and rejection count.
- **Friday — Build note:** one release change, screenshot or reproducibility improvement.
- **Monthly — Long-form thread:** one complete workflow from data intake to robustness review.

## Post template

**Hook:** one concrete observation.  
**Context:** instrument, timeframe, period and sample size.  
**Method:** gates, costs, IS/OOS and robustness checks.  
**Result:** measured values, including drawdown and trade count.  
**Limitation:** what the test does not prove.  
**CTA:** link to the full methodology or report.

## Example

> We tested [N] candidates on [instrument/timeframe].
>
> Only [M] passed the documented IS/OOS gates.
>
> The shortlist was then checked with [walk-forward method] and [Monte Carlo setup].
>
> Costs: [spread], [commission], [slippage].
>
> The result is historical research — not a promise. Full packet: [link]

## Editorial rules

- Never imply guaranteed returns or guaranteed evaluations.
- Never publish a performance number without its period and costs.
- Separate historical, paper and live evidence.
- Use screenshots of reports and cite the assumptions.
- Link to source documents and invite technical questions.
