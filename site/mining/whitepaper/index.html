Technical Whitepaper

SYNBAI Mining: Project Overview, Prototype Status & Planned Solana Integrations.

1. Abstract & Ecosystem Vision

SYNBAI Mining is developing a trading-signal community, website dashboard, strategy catalog, and planned Solana token ecosystem. The current website and API are prototypes. Strategy performance, token rewards, and yield are not independently verified or guaranteed.

2. Research Direction

The project intends to explore quantitative strategy tooling and risk controls. No claim is made here that a specific strategy is profitable, audited, or ready for live trading.

  • Strategy research: MQL5 and quantitative research tools are under development; generated or stored strategies require independent validation.
  • Risk disclosure: Trading can result in partial or total loss. Historical or simulated results do not establish future performance.

3. Validation Status

A reproducible, independently verified quantitative validation pipeline is not currently established for all strategies shown in the prototype. No strategy metrics in the dashboard should be treated as audited results.

  • Future performance reports should identify source data, assumptions, fees, slippage, time periods, and reproducible test procedures.
  • Database scores or filters alone are not proof that a strategy passed a financial validation process.

4. Wallet and On-Chain Status

A wallet message-signature sign-in is an authentication feature; it does not itself transfer tokens, verify payment, mint an NFT, stake assets, or claim rewards. Some API records are off-chain prototypes. No verified SYNBAI Mainnet mint, pool, staking contract, or reward contract is active.

The project currently has a Devnet SPL token for testing. Devnet tokens have no Mainnet value and do not migrate automatically. Mainnet supply, authorities, allocations, vesting, pool parameters, and legal disclosures remain subject to final implementation and review.

5. Project Materials

This page is a project overview, not an investment offer or audited white paper. Production source code, private strategy files, wallet keypairs, credentials, and user databases are not included here. Follow the official project channels for verified status updates.

Do not rely on this document as a promise of token value, price appreciation, exchange listing, liquidity, trading profit, or future functionality.
