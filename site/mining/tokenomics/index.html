$SYNBAI Tokenomics

NON-LIVE PROPOSAL. This document does not authorize deployment, minting, a sale, pool creation, or any transaction. No SYNBAI Mainnet token, presale, or Mainnet liquidity pool is confirmed active. Devnet tokens have no Mainnet value and do not migrate automatically.

03 / PRICE SCENARIO

Proposed price and full sell-out math

Proposed sale price

Base price without discount: 1,000,000 SYNBAI per SOL (0.000001 SOL per SYNBAI). A fixed 25% discount is proposed for the entire sale, replacing the former five-tier discount ladder.

Effective proposal: 0.00000075 SOL / SYNBAI
1,333,333.33 SYNBAI / SOL

At a complete 150M sale

Gross proceeds: 112.5 SOL before fees and rent. The proposed LP quote is 65% of gross proceeds: 73.125 SOL, paired with 97,500,000 SYNBAI.

The pool is proposed to open at the same effective price as the sale. The remaining 52,500,000 SYNBAI in the LP bucket moves to In-game NFT rewards.

Sell-out figures apply only to a full sale at the stated reference price. Recalculate against actual receipts and the actual SOL price. If the LP rule is applied to net rather than gross proceeds, the SOL quote and LP token amount decrease. No pool is active.

04 / NFT REWARDS

In-game NFT rewards proposal

  • At sell-out, the variable allocation is 152,500,000 SYNBAI: 100,000,000 base plus 52,500,000 potential LP surplus.
  • Proposed monthly distribution over 24 months. The cap is 25,000 SYNBAI per active NFT per month.
  • If a monthly tranche cannot cover all caps, distribution is pro-rata. Unused amounts roll over to the following month.
  • A proposed 5% burn applies automatically when a wallet withdraws tokens mined with an NFT. No price impact is promised.
  • No yield is promised. Rigs, hash rates, and mining are simulations; the exact hash formula remains to be defined.

INACTIVE: NFT minting, mining-token withdrawals, and reward distribution are not live.

05 / COMMUNITY

Founder Pass and airdrop proposal

  • Founder 3% + Airdrops 2% = 5% (50,000,000 SYNBAI). This replaces the former Founder Passes 5% allocation; it is not additional to Rewards.
  • Founder Pass owners are proposed to be whitelisted for a free mint of 300 NFTs, limited to one per wallet and IP. The IP restriction can be bypassed.
  • VIP Passes and NFTs are proposed to be soulbound. Soulbound status prevents transfer, but does not remove every possible form of value or utility.
  • The A1 document reports, according to the owner, that higher SYNBAI NEXUS AI usage limits are functional on Oracle with Qwen ported 2B; this has not been independently verified.
  • The airdrop is not guaranteed and is not live. Founder Pass waitlist concepts do not imply an active sale or mint.
06 / WAVE 2

NFT boxes proposal

A proposal for 1,000 NFT boxes after the Wave 1 free mint, for VIP Pass holders and other eligible participants. Proposed price: 0.10 SOL per box. At sell-out, gross proceeds would be 100 SOL before fees and rent.

The proposed split applies to net proceeds N, after fees and rent: 50% SYNBAI buyback, 10% marketing, 10% Founder fee, 10% development team, and 20% operations. A buyback is an intention; no price increase is promised. Box price and details remain editable until Mainnet.

07 / OPEN ITEMS

Details still to be decided

  • Net proceeds after fees and rent; final LP surplus after actual receipts and SOL price.
  • Wave 2 box contents and utility; Founder Pass NFT collection metadata, mint mechanics, rent, and eligibility.
  • Actual demand for 1,000 boxes at 0.10 SOL; legal and tax treatment of buybacks, Founder fees, airdrops, and NFT sales.
  • Sale mode (Pro Rata, Fixed Price, or FCFS), soft/hard caps, fees, vesting, and wallet addresses are outside A1 and are not fixed here.
  • Rigs per user relative to total users, individual hash and network total remain TBD; rig and hash figures are simulated.
  • A1 uses 24 months (two years). The separate “several years” duration target has not been defined numerically.
  • Supply, decimals, allocations, and all launch mechanics remain proposals and have not been verified on-chain.
