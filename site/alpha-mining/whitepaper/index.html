Project notes • living document

Research with clear boundaries.

SYNBAI Mining is an early-stage project exploring systematic trading research tools and a potential Solana-based community layer. This page distinguishes the current prototype from future plans.

Status: The SYNBAI mint known to the project is on Devnet. Mainnet launch, token trading, NFT issuance, staking, rewards and Staff vesting are not represented as live here.

Product direction

The software direction is to support the creation, organization and evaluation of algorithmic trading strategies, including workflows associated with MetaTrader 5. Automated strategies carry risk: software can malfunction, market conditions change, and historical tests do not guarantee future results. No strategy or yield is promised.

Quantitative claims such as validation scores, drawdown limits, Monte Carlo run counts or live performance will be published only alongside their methodology, data period, assumptions, limitations and reproducible evidence. Until that evidence is published, such numbers should not be interpreted as independently verified results.

Private strategy assets

Strategy source files and unreleased strategy names, parameters, scores, test results and metadata are proprietary project assets. They should not be committed to a public Git repository, exposed through public APIs, included in open NFT metadata, or placed in public build artifacts. Access controls must be enforced server-side; hiding content in the browser is not a security boundary.

Solana ecosystem plan

The project is considering SYNBAI as an ecosystem token and may later explore NFT access products and utility. The current planned allocation is documented separately in Tokenomics. Plans are not guarantees, and any Mainnet functionality depends on implementation, operational readiness and public disclosure.

Planned Staff vesting: Jupiter Lock on Solana Mainnet, with a proposed 24-month schedule and equal monthly unlocks. The schedule becomes verifiable only after its Mainnet lock and transaction are created and independently inspected.

Security and release principles

  • Never ask users to provide seed phrases or private keys.
  • Wallet signatures should be limited to clearly described authentication or transaction actions.
  • Do not label an off-chain database record as an NFT, token transfer, staking position or on-chain reward.
  • Publish mint, lock, liquidity and treasury references only after checking them on the correct network.
  • Keep production secrets and proprietary strategy files outside public repositories and static web roots.

Risk notice

Digital assets and algorithmic trading can result in a total loss. Token liquidity, utility, market access and future development are uncertain. This document is not investment, legal, tax or financial advice and does not promise returns.
