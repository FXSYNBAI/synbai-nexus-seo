Before installation

What you need.

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • A verified SYNBAI account to activate the research trial.
  • Historical market CSV data for your research.
  • Internet access for account activation and online services.
  • A separately installed Ollama model if you want local AI assistance.
