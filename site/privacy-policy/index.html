SYNBAI / Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SYNBAI Mining handles information when you use the Mining and Alpha Mining pages, wallet sign-in, and related account features on fxsynbai.eu.

Effective date: November 15, 2026

1. Who operates the service

SYNBAI Mining is operated by PLOESTEANU DAN VICTOR PFA. For privacy questions or data requests, contact lupoaicadacica@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Google sign-in: When you choose Google sign-in, the authentication service requests the OpenID Connect openid and email scopes. It receives your Google account identifier and verified email address to authenticate you and maintain your SYNBAI account. It does not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, Drive, or other Google content.
  • Wallet sign-in and linking: We process the public Solana wallet address and a signed, one-time message used to prove control of that address. The message-signing step does not submit a blockchain transaction. We do not ask for or receive your seed phrase or wallet private key.
  • Waitlist and Alpha: If you join the Founder Pass waitlist, we process the public wallet address, your selected pass preference, and an optional community code. Alpha demo actions and simulated rig or credit state may be associated with that wallet address.
  • Technical and security data: Hosting and API providers may process IP address, browser or device information, request time, and diagnostic records to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot failures.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, link a wallet when you request it, manage waitlist entries and simulated Alpha state, enforce usage limits, secure the service, respond to support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and local storage

Sign-in uses secure, HTTP-only session cookies and temporary authentication-state cookies where enabled. These cookies support account security and login completion. Blocking them may prevent sign-in from working. Wallet extensions operate under their own privacy policies and permissions.

5. How information is shared

We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the service, including Google for sign-in, Cloudflare for website delivery and protection, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure for the game API and wallet-authenticated services. Wallet providers receive information directly when you interact with their extension. We do not sell Google user data, and we do not use it for advertising. Google account data is used only for sign-in and account features.

6. Retention and security

Account and wallet-link records are retained while needed to provide your account and its requested features. Temporary authentication challenges expire and are removed as part of security cleanup. Operational records and usage totals may be retained for service security, quotas, and troubleshooting. We use access controls and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You may stop using Google sign-in and revoke SYNBAI's Google connection from your Google Account security settings. To ask us to access, correct, unlink, or delete information associated with your account, email lupoaicadacica@gmail.com from the account address or include the relevant public wallet address. We may ask for reasonable proof that you control the account or wallet before acting on a request.

8. International processing

Our infrastructure providers may process information in the regions where they operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border data handling.

9. Children

The service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 18.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above shows when this version was prepared. Questions and requests can be sent to lupoaicadacica@gmail.com.
