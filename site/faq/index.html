SYNBAI Nexus / Support

Frequently asked questions.

Understand the workflow, installation and account options before your first research run.

Does SYNBAI Nexus trade on my behalf?

No. The desktop research engine runs historical simulations. Exported scripts require your own review, compilation and testing before any subsequent use.

Does it guarantee profits or a prop-firm challenge pass?

No. Historical results and robustness checks cannot guarantee future performance. Review every strategy against your broker conditions and applicable prop-firm rules.

How does the trial work?

A verified account starts a 15-day trial without a payment card. The trial includes limited testing and export allowances. Review the full plan comparison for current limits.

What do I need to get started?

A Windows 10 or 11 64-bit computer, a SYNBAI account and suitable historical market data. See the download page for the installation workflow.

Where can I get help?

Use the product documentation, website AI Support or email support@fxsynbai.eu. AI-generated explanations should be checked against the product and your research context.

Which historical data is included?

The release is being prepared with XAUUSD M1 OHLC history covering 17 November 2025–13 November 2026. The desktop engine can aggregate M1 history into H1 strategy bars. You can import your own suitable MT5 historical CSV data.

Is local AI the same as website AI Support?

No. The desktop app can connect to a local Ollama model. Website AI Support is a separate online service with account-plan limits.

Where can I compare plans and billing terms?

See the Pricing page for current capacity, billing options and plan limits, and the refund policy before purchasing.
