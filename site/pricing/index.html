Straightforward terms

Billing, in brief

Know how renewal, cancellation and the first-purchase guarantee work.

Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions renew at the displayed monthly price until you cancel. Cancel any time; access continues through the paid period.

Annual plans

Annual subscriptions are paid upfront once per year at the displayed annual price. The annual discount is calculated against 12 monthly payments.

30-day first-purchase guarantee

For the first paid subscription purchase on an account, request a refund within 30 calendar days after payment. The initial plan payment is refunded and the subscription is canceled. Mandatory consumer rights are unaffected.

Payment methods

Stripe Checkout shows payment options available in your location. Crypto payments are accepted in USDC or SOL on Solana; crypto renewals are manual and network fees may apply.

Lifetime plans — single one-time payment

Pay once for Starter Lifetime or Pro Lifetime to unlock the selected plan permanently. There are no monthly or annual renewals and no recurring subscription charges.

Questions

Before you choose

Quick answers about access, research and changing plans.

Does Pro make the strategy search inherently better?

All plans use the same research foundation. Pro raises research quotas and unlocks configurable genetic search controls.

When does my trial start?

Your 15-day trial starts automatically after you verify your account email.

Can I change or cancel my subscription?

Use Manage Subscription in your account to manage the subscription. Monthly plans can be canceled any time; access continues through the current paid period.

Is strategy performance guaranteed?

No. Historical testing, Monte Carlo analysis and validation results are research tools, not a forecast or promise of future performance.

Where does strategy testing run?

Testing runs in the desktop SYNBAI Nexus app on your computer. The website manages your account, plan and app download.
