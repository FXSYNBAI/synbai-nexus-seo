SYNBAI Mining Roadmap

Product readiness, transparent data, and staged Solana development

Current Phase

Prototype Stabilization & Data Integrity

  • Website, API, and Discord: Services are deployed; dashboard and API features still include prototypes.
  • Honest dashboard data: Remove fabricated counters, distinguish off-chain records from NFTs, and reconcile database schemas and paths.
  • Wallet and Founder Pass: Keep sales and claims disabled until authentication, payment verification, and real minting are implemented.
  • Strategy validation: Publish performance metrics only with reproducible source data and documented methodology.
  • Token status: SYNBAI exists on Devnet for development. No verified Mainnet mint or liquidity pool is active.
After prototype remediation

Mainnet Launch Gates

  • Finalize token metadata, custody, legal review, and a separate Mainnet mint.
  • Verify the fixed supply and all allocations, then revoke mint authority according to the launch plan.
  • Deploy the Staff allocation to Jupiter Lock with 24 equal monthly tranches and verify it on-chain.
  • Choose liquidity amounts, initial price, pool ownership, fee rights, and LP lock duration before funding.
  • Review a Meteora DAMM v2 dry-run using the approved exponential 10% to 1% fee schedule over 10 minutes; create a pool only after signer review.
Future, subject to funding and implementation

Product Utility

  • Define the 1,000 planned free VIP Box contents, eligibility, claim process, and fulfillment before enabling access.
  • Build staking only with pre-funded, capped rewards and clearly disclosed costs.
  • Publish only verified catalog records, wallet entitlements, and transaction references.
Later, only after operating product is established

Longer-Term Options

  • Consider governance only after rights and responsibilities are defined.
  • Consider buyback or burn mechanisms only with a verified source of funds and public rules.
  • Evaluate listings and partnerships when the product, disclosures, and liquidity are ready. No listing is promised.
